How to interpret the results

Investment: purchased challenges × (challenge fee + optional fees). Expected collected payout: accounts × Phase 1 pass × Phase 2 pass × funded target success × collection probability × (net reward + eligible fee refund).

Net profit: expected receipts − total challenge spending. Net ROI: net profit ÷ total spending. 3× net: after receiving payouts, net profit equals at least three times what you spent, requiring total receipts ≥4× spending.

Pass rates default to earlier hypothetical model estimates. A simulation produces another estimate under simplified assumptions—it does not make historical or real-world payout probabilities certain. The dashboard does not include exact FTMO spreads, swaps, partial closures, news events, slippage, external trading-platform lot restrictions or all program rules.

For official conditions, check FTMO Trading Objectives and FTMO FAQ before buying or routing live trades.
