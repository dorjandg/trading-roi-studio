Uses the Trade Lab's account selection, horizon, WR, RR, risk and loss rules.
This is a simplified routing model—not an instruction to risk an account's full daily loss allowance. Committed-risk mode deducts the full potential risk of each new trade for the day, even following winning trades. Net daily loss mode allows replenishment after profitable trades but prevents an additional trade whose full planned risk would exceed the selected daily loss budget. Signals are not duplicated to every account: one trade idea is allocated to one eligible account. Risk headroom and stage progress are approximate; choosing a priority changes which accounts receive signals but does not prove that policy is optimal. Selected firm limits, trailing excursion rules, account caps, evaluation transitions and payout requirements are not all enforced. Topstep Trading Combines are not capped here, but active Topstep XFAs are limited to five. FTMO scenarios above $400K total allocation are blocked. Other funded account structures and rules remain approximate.

Portfolio cash-equity path

Modeled net cash = funded withdrawals after the configured trader split plus modeled refunds, minus purchase, renewal, and activation fees. Evaluation profits are not paid cash. Model notes

A win earns risk × RR minus trade cost; a loss loses risk plus cost; BE pays trade cost only. Win probability and BE probability are exclusive categories. For example, 35% wins and 5% BE means 60% losses. Fixed dollar risk is used, subject to the simplified account-loss stop. Topstep combines use an end-of-day trailing balance floor capped at the starting equity; daily-loss stops (if configured) pause until the next modeled day. FTMO daily loss is approximated from the start of each modeled day and breaches the path. Best-day consistency uses simulated daily closing P/L. A breached path stops trading. Month boundaries divide the requested number of trades evenly into 20 simulated trading days per month. Daily equity candles are based on those modeled trade days, not real historical trading data.

This simulator does not model intratrade excursions, fees tied to contract size, margin, exact exchange sessions, platform rule variants, phase-to-phase transitions, payout cycles, market-dependent win rates, or correlated strategies. FTMO daily rules are approximated from modeled daily starting equity; Topstep's optional daily-loss setting must be entered for the chosen account. Use it for exploratory statistics, not prop-firm compliance or investment advice.
