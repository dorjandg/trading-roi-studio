Model notes

A win earns risk × RR minus trade cost; a loss loses risk plus cost; BE pays trade cost only. Win probability and BE probability are exclusive categories. For example, 35% wins and 5% BE means 60% losses. Fixed dollar risk is used, subject to the simplified account-loss stop. A breached path stops trading. Month boundaries divide the requested number of trades evenly into 20 simulated trading days per month. Daily equity candles are based on those modeled trade days, not real historical trading data.

This simulator does not yet enforce firm-specific intraday daily loss limits, payout days, trailing intratrade excursions, position margin, commissions by lot size, market-dependent win rates, or correlated strategies. Use it for exploratory statistics, not prop-firm compliance or investment advice.
