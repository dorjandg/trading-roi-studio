### Suggested short biography, English

Landi Kademi brings decades of live performance experience to the craft of vocal coaching. His television work includes preparing contestants for X Factor Albania and supporting Xuxi’s team as an assistant coach on The Voice of Albania. At Vocal Mastery, that experience informs a personal approach to technique, musical expression and confidence on stage.
