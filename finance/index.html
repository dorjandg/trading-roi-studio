Financial reconciliation

Review possible duplicate entries, unmatched payouts and missing cash records

Review suggestions · No automatic changes

Potential duplicate transactions

Same owner, date, type, amount, currency and normalized provider. Review before deleting.

Prop firm payouts not matched to income transactions

Matches on owner, provider, currency and payout amount. A matching entry is evidence to review, not confirmation of bank settlement.

Prop firm fees not matched to expense transactions

Evaluation and activation fees are compared against recorded prop-firm expenses. Multiple fees can share amounts; unmatched entries are suggestions, not proof of missing payments.

Reconciliation rules

No transactions are created, merged or deleted automatically. This screen compares manually entered local records and may show false positives. Account payout dates are used where available; older records may lack them. Transactions without a provider or with alternate provider spellings may not match. Use original invoices and payout confirmations before correcting records.

Accounting notes

Prop firm ROI is calculated from manually entered payout cash less evaluation and activation fees, divided by those fees. It excludes trading P&L and any costs recorded only in Transactions. Avoid entering the same fee twice when reconciling. For legacy account records without a payout receipt date, the account start date is used as a fallback. Period ROI is indicative because fees and payouts may fall in different periods. Subscription forecasts are estimates, not booked expenses.

Financial accounting rules

Net cash = payouts received − evaluation and activation/reset fees. Account size is not an asset balance. Challenge profit targets and simulated trading profits are not cash income. Each record is manually entered and may require reconciliation against invoices or payout statements.

Data & privacy

This version stores data in this browser only. It does not provide user authentication, shared storage, encryption-at-rest on a server or automatic backups. Export your records regularly.

A full backup includes transactions, prop accounts, subscriptions and invoices. Restoring replaces the local records in this browser after confirmation.
