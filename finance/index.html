Accounting notes

Prop firm ROI is calculated from manually entered payout cash less evaluation and activation fees, divided by those fees. It excludes trading P&L and any costs recorded only in Transactions. Avoid entering the same fee twice when reconciling. For legacy account records without a payout receipt date, the account start date is used as a fallback. Period ROI is indicative because fees and payouts may fall in different periods. Subscription forecasts are estimates, not booked expenses.

Order confirmations alone are not expenses. A refund sent is not a received refund. USDT is not USD; do not convert without evidence. For confirmed payments, choose the correct FTMO account and verify it is not already included in the account's evaluation fee before adding an event.
