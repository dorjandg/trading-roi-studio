5 · Portfolio size, timing & formulas Show / hide
P(funded) = P(P1) × P(P2 conditional). P(receive payout) = P(funded) × P(funded target) × P(realize cash after milestone).
Net cash = expected cash payouts + first-payout refunds − purchase fees − funded activation costs. ROI = net cash ÷ total costs. Gross multiple = total cash receipts ÷ total costs.
Cost / funded = purchase fee ÷ funded conversion + extra cost on funded. Cost / first payout = total costs ÷ expected first-payout events. Trade EV (R) = win rate × RR − (1 − win rate).

Calculated averages for a cohort of purchases, not promises that fractional accounts or payouts will occur. Refunds are counted only on first successful FTMO payouts. Subsequent payouts, recurring funded lifetime, unfinished attempts, renewal fees, slippage, taxes and discounting are not included. Weekly results appear only when you supply a calendar horizon; simply dividing by weeks does not simulate a trading schedule.
