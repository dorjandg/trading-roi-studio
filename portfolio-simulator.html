Options-based Monte Carlo · cash risk · first payout

Budget → Challenges → Phases → Payout

Uses the saved RR, R1/R2/R3 and risk decisions from your account's phase options. It simulates random trade outcomes rather than choosing future winning paths. No orders are placed.

Model scope: Risk amounts are used exactly or the trade is blocked. Minimum trading days after reaching a target are represented as administrative qualification assumptions (not invented W/L trades). Payout eligibility, subscriptions, trailing losses, firm allocation and checkout rules need separate verification.

1 · Investment & simulation inputs

Same-budget comparison allocates the entire budget separately to each firm. Split-budget mode divides one budget across the selected firms.

Dynamic: buy on a trading day only if an open slot and sufficient available money exist. After an account fails, exhausts its risk, or gets paid, its slot can be reused the next trading day. Daily trade opportunities are assigned across available accounts. In split-budget mode they are also distributed across the selected firms, so opportunities are not multiplied. Replacement purchases use available budget, not unlimited credit.

In compare mode each firm uses its own hypothetical budget and trade schedule. In split-budget mode the dynamic daily trade opportunities are shared among firms. Do not sum independent comparisons.

Model assumptions & limits

Read before interpreting an ROI estimate

Challenge purchases follow the selected buying mode: upfront on day 0, or dynamic day-by-day purchases and replacements when trading slots and budget permit. Dynamic replacements respond to failed, risk-exhausted, or first-paid accounts; minimum-day and funded eligibility assumptions still apply. Taxes, automatic refund processing delays, and repeated funded payouts are not simulated. Topstep's subscription renewals, variant-specific activation rules, contract limits and full payout eligibility are not enforced. Its modeled initial payout is up to 50% of realized funded profit capped at $5,000; check the exact purchased path. FTMO/Hola pay a modeled split of funded profit after a planning target and waiting period, not a guaranteed withdrawal. Trailing drawdown is approximated for Topstep using end-of-day high-water marks and a zero-capped floor; intraday breaches and contract-size restrictions are not verified. Other firms' static initial floors are modeled where known. Fees, spread/slippage, evaluation activation delays, minimum qualifying trades and news restrictions may differ. If multiple accounts share a signal, outcomes may be correlated and diversification overstated. The model's risk-exhausted status does not mean an official failed-account breach.

Research tool only. Sourced catalog rules are snapshots, not live purchase/eligibility confirmations. The simulator reads profiles locally; budget changes here don't overwrite the main Overview settings. The maximum of 100 modeled challenges per account type is a performance cap, not a prop-firm permission limit.
