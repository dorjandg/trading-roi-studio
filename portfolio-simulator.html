Budget → Challenges → Phases → Payout

Uses each account’s saved risk policy from Firms: fixed R1–R4, or FTMO’s optional dynamic Funded Buffer Builder. Targets and loss limits remain specific to each firm and phase. Trade outcomes are simulated, never predicted; no orders are placed.

Model scope: Risk amounts are used exactly or the trade is blocked. Minimum trading days after reaching a target are represented as administrative qualification assumptions (not invented W/L trades). Payout eligibility, subscriptions, trailing losses, firm allocation and checkout rules need separate verification.

Model assumptions & limits
Read before interpreting an ROI estimate

Challenge purchases follow the selected buying mode: upfront on day 0, or dynamic day-by-day purchases and replacements when trading slots and budget permit. Dynamic replacements respond to failed, risk-exhausted, or first-paid accounts; minimum-day and funded eligibility assumptions still apply. Taxes, automatic refund processing delays, and repeated funded payouts are not simulated. Topstep's subscription renewals, variant-specific activation rules, contract limits and full payout eligibility are not enforced. Its modeled initial payout is up to 50% of realized funded profit capped at $5,000; check the exact purchased path. FTMO/Hola pay a modeled split of funded profit after a planning target and waiting period, not a guaranteed withdrawal. Topstep Standard Trading Combine → Express Funded is modeled as exactly two stages; later optional Live Funded promotion is outside this first-payout model. Topstep's MLL is an end-of-day trailing loss floor, updated upward after each session's closing balance and permanently locked at $0 once reached. The floor is monitored against modeled realized trade outcomes; unrealized intratrade breaches and contract-size restrictions are not verified. Other firms' static initial floors are modeled where known. The funded target is a threshold, not a cap on the final trade: a completed trade can finish above it. FTMO/Hola funded accounts stop receiving further strategy trades at that threshold, then wait for the first-funded-trade payout eligibility clock and any modeled processing days. Topstep can require further qualifying winning days. Evaluation minimum-size qualification orders are synthetic placeholders (0.01 lot for CFD programs, 1 micro for Topstep) and have no modeled P/L, trading costs or actual lot eligibility: real minimum-size trades can gain or lose money. Calendar waits are approximated using trading days. Fees, spread/slippage, evaluation activation delays and news restrictions may differ. If multiple accounts share a signal, outcomes may be correlated and diversification overstated. The model's risk-exhausted status does not mean an official failed-account breach.

Research tool only. Sourced catalog rules are snapshots, not live purchase/eligibility confirmations. The simulator reads profiles locally; budget changes here don't overwrite the main Overview settings. The maximum of 100 modeled challenges per account type is a performance cap, not a prop-firm permission limit.
